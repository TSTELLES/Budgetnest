Privacy Policy — BudgetNest

Last Updated: September 15, 2026

1. Introduction

BudgetNest is a personal finance and budgeting application developed by T S Telles.

BudgetNest helps users organize personal financial information, including expenses, budgets, savings, investments, subscriptions, shopping lists, credit cards, reminders, and related preferences.

This Privacy Policy explains what information BudgetNest processes, how that information is used, how it is stored, when it may be shared with service providers, and how users can request deletion of their account and associated data.

By using BudgetNest, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Process

Depending on the features you use, BudgetNest may process the following information.

Account and Authentication Information

  • Email address
  • Firebase user ID (UID)
  • Authentication status
  • Email verification status
  • Account plan information, such as Free or Premium status

BudgetNest uses Firebase Authentication for account creation, login, password recovery, and email verification.

Users may be required to verify their email address before accessing the main application.

Information You Provide

  • Preferred name
  • Monthly budget
  • Payday
  • Country or region
  • Currency
  • Financial goals
  • Expenses
  • Income-related information
  • Budget information
  • Credit card information
  • Installment information
  • Subscription information
  • Investment information
  • Emergency reserve information
  • Shopping and grocery information
  • Pet-related expense information
  • Child-related expense information
  • Planned spending information
  • Notification preferences
  • Theme preferences
  • Accessibility preferences

3. How We Use Information

  • create and authenticate your account;
  • verify your email address;
  • allow password recovery;
  • provide budgeting and financial organization features;
  • calculate and display financial summaries;
  • remember application preferences;
  • provide regional and currency-related functionality;
  • provide supported reminders and notifications;
  • provide advertising on applicable plans;
  • maintain and manage the user's selected account plan;
  • provide customer and privacy support;
  • process account and data deletion requests.

BudgetNest does not use your financial information to provide banking, lending, investment-management, or payment-processing services.

4. Financial Information and Local Storage

BudgetNest allows users to record financial information such as expenses, budgets, cards, installments, subscriptions, investments, and emergency reserves.

Substantial portions of this financial information are stored locally on the user's device using the application's local storage system rather than being stored as complete financial records in the BudgetNest Firebase backend.

Local financial data is used to provide the application's budgeting and organization features.

Users should avoid entering information into BudgetNest that they do not wish to store on their device.

5. Firebase Services

Firebase Authentication

Firebase Authentication is used for:

  • account creation;
  • email/password login;
  • email verification;
  • password recovery;
  • authentication management.

Firebase Firestore

Firestore is used for certain account-related information, including account plan information.

6. Email Verification

BudgetNest may require users who create an account to verify their email address before accessing the main application.

The verification process uses Firebase Authentication.

BudgetNest does not receive or store the user's email account password in plaintext.

7. Password Recovery

BudgetNest provides a password-recovery process through Firebase Authentication.

When a user requests a password reset, Firebase may send a password-reset email to the address associated with the account.

BudgetNest does not display or store the user's password in plaintext.

8. Advertising and AdMob

BudgetNest uses Google AdMob for advertising on applicable plans.

The Free version may display advertisements, including banner advertisements and certain rewarded advertisements.

Advertising services may process advertising-related information, including the Android Advertising ID, according to Google's applicable policies.

BudgetNest does not sell users' personal or financial information.

9. Regional Information and Payment Methods

BudgetNest allows users to select a country or region and currency.

Certain application options may vary according to the selected country, including available payment-method categories and certain fixed-bill options.

This information is used to provide the application's regional functionality.

BudgetNest does not use this functionality to connect the user to a bank or payment network.

10. Currency and Exchange Rates

BudgetNest provides currency-related functionality and may retrieve exchange-rate information from an external exchange-rate service over HTTPS.

The application may process the selected currency and requested exchange-rate information to provide this feature.

11. Open Finance

The current version of BudgetNest includes an Open Finance interface, but it is not currently a live banking connection.

The current implementation does not establish live connections to users' bank accounts or retrieve actual banking transactions through Open Finance. The feature currently uses an in-app mock/placeholder implementation.

12. Notifications

BudgetNest may request permission to send notifications.

Supported notifications may include reminders relating to supported financial planning features.

Some notification categories may be displayed in the application's settings but may not currently generate notifications.

Users can manage notification permissions through Android system settings and the applicable BudgetNest settings.

13. Android Permissions

  • Internet access — authentication, advertising, and exchange-rate services;
  • Notification permission — supported notifications;
  • Receive boot completed — rescheduling applicable reminders;
  • Display over other apps — optional floating calculator functionality.

14. Data Sharing

BudgetNest does not sell personal or sensitive user information.

Information may be processed by service providers including:

  • Google Firebase — authentication and account-related services;
  • Google AdMob — advertising and advertising-related services;
  • Exchange-rate provider — exchange-rate requests made through the currency feature.

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

BudgetNest's current Open Finance implementation does not transmit the user's financial records to a bank or financial institution.

15. Data Security

BudgetNest uses reasonable technical measures appropriate to the application's architecture to protect information.

Information transmitted to external services is intended to use secure connections such as HTTPS where supported.

Authentication is handled through Firebase Authentication rather than storing user passwords directly in the application's local financial data.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

16. Account Deletion

BudgetNest provides an account-deletion option for users who have created an account.

The deletion process is intended to:

  1. require explicit user confirmation;
  2. delete the Firebase Authentication account;
  3. remove associated account data maintained by BudgetNest, where technically applicable;
  4. remove the application's locally stored user data associated with the account;
  5. sign the user out;
  6. return the user to the appropriate initial application flow.

Account deletion is permanent and may not be reversible.

Where certain information must legally or legitimately be retained, such retention will be limited to the applicable purpose and period.

Account Deletion Web Request

To request deletion of your BudgetNest account and associated data, please contact:

autoratstelles@gmail.com

17. Data Retention

Account information maintained through Firebase may remain associated with the user's account until the account is deleted or the information is otherwise removed in accordance with applicable requirements.

Locally stored financial and preference information remains on the device until deleted through the application's deletion process, Android application-data removal, device reset, or another applicable mechanism.

Some information may be retained where necessary for legitimate legal, security, fraud-prevention, or regulatory purposes.

18. Children's Privacy

BudgetNest is a personal finance application and is not specifically directed to children.

The application may allow an adult user to record information related to children as part of family budgeting features. This does not mean that children are intended to create or manage BudgetNest accounts.

Users should not provide unnecessary personal information about other individuals.

19. Third-Party Services

  • Google Firebase Authentication
  • Google Firebase Firestore
  • Google AdMob
  • An external exchange-rate service

Third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

20. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary to reflect changes to BudgetNest, applicable laws, third-party services, or data practices.

When changes are made, the "Last Updated" date will be updated.
