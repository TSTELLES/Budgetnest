BudgetNest — Account and Data Deletion

This page provides a way to request deletion of your BudgetNest account and associated data.

Important: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone once completed.

What will be deleted?

When a valid deletion request is processed, BudgetNest will delete the account associated with the request and the user data that BudgetNest maintains and can delete as part of the account-deletion process.

This may include account authentication information and associated BudgetNest account data. Financial and preference information stored locally by the application will also be removed through the application's account-deletion process where applicable.

Certain information may be retained when retention is required or permitted for legitimate legal, security, fraud-prevention, or regulatory purposes.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email from the address associated with your BudgetNest account.
  2. Use the subject: BudgetNest Account Deletion Request
  3. Include the email address associated with your BudgetNest account.
  4. Submit the request using the email address below.
